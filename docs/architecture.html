Technical overview

Service architecture

The codebase is one part of a larger XGame/Tars environment. Requests move from tournament clients through RPC services into game-state, club, event, configuration, and persistence components.

01

Client layer

Tournament and management interfaces consume service APIs.

02

Tars services

RPC, routing, servant implementations, configuration, and logs.

03

Domain logic

Rooms, players, tournament rules, clubs, events, and rankings.

04

Data layer

MySQL, generated protocols, and external XGame modules.

Repository boundaries

  • core/ contains room and player-state related logic.
  • GMServer.*, GMServantImp.*, and gameserver.* provide service components.
  • .tars and protocol resources describe service contracts.
  • External shared modules and generated headers are not fully included.

Production considerations

Before deployment, review authentication, authorization, secret storage, encryption, logging, privacy retention, protocol compatibility, database migrations, monitoring, backups, rate limits, and regional legal requirements.
