Platform capabilities

Tournament features

Server-side building blocks for live Texas Hold'em events, from tournament state and player seating to club, ranking, registration, and ticket integrations.

Tournament formats

Structures for Sit & Go, multi-table tournaments, Freezeout events, and ranking leagues. Exact business rules depend on the surrounding XGame services and configuration.

Room and player state

Core logic covers rooms, tables, seating, stand-up, offline handling, and player information used by tournament services.

Club and rankings

Tars and protocol resources support clubs, activities, friends, points, rankings, and event-facing service integration.

Registration and tickets

Protocol foundations connect qualification, enrollment, ticketing, and event operations. Payment and compliance systems are deployment-specific.

Service integration

Tars RPC, Protobuf resources, MySQL client integration, RapidJSON, configuration, and logging support a service-oriented backend.

Responsible scope

The project is intended for lawful event management, research, and demonstration. Commercial deployment requires separate licensing.
